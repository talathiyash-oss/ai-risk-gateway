# Interview Cheat Sheet: Daniel (CISO)

## Remember
- Listen more than you talk.
- No solutions today. Only understanding.
- After he answers, wait 2 seconds.

## The 3 phrases (use after every answer)
1. **"Tell me more about that."**
2. **"Why does that matter?"** (up to 3 times)
3. **"Can you give me an example?"**

## Opening (say it word for word)
"Thanks for making time, Daniel. I'd like to spend about 20 minutes understanding what you're dealing with around the copilot rollout. No solutions today, I just want to understand the problem. Is that okay?"

## Questions
1. What's going on with the copilot rollout from your side?
2. What's the hardest part of what the Risk Committee asked you for?
3. Tell me about the incident in October.
4. How do you handle AI risk today?
5. What would a good outcome look like in March?
6. What would a bad outcome look like?
7. What are you working with in terms of budget, time and people?
8. Who else cares about this, and who might push back?

**Before closing, check you have:** his real problem, what the committee will accept, an acceptable loss level (ask for a number!), budget, and who else to talk to.

## Closing
"Let me play back what I heard: ___. Did I get that right? What did I miss? And who else should I talk to?"

Then say **"end interview"** for the debrief.

## If stuck
| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| Mind goes blank | "Let me make sure I understood. You said…" |
| Short answer | "Tell me more about that." |
| He pitches a solution | "What would that let you do?" |
| You asked a bad question | Just ask the next one |
